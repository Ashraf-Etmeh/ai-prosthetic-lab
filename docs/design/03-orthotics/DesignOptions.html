Hinged AFO, plantarflexion stop

Stop set at the calf length, dorsiflexion free

Addresses

R1 yes R2 yes R3 yes R4 partly R5 adjustable R6 with lining R7 partly

Costs the patient

  • Bulkier at the ankle
  • Joints need checking and repair
  • Heavier than option A

Evidence

Guideline · strong · AFO for gait speed in chronic stroke Guideline · AFO types not compared Practice · design trade-offs, no outcome evidence

Materials and parts for this case

  • Polypropylene shell with adjustable ankle joints
  • Joint axis aligned to the malleoli
  • Soft lining: reduced sensation
  • D-ring strap for one hand
Clinic stock: joints in stock

Check at fitting

  • Skin over the joints after 20 minutes
