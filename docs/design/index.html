SYSTEM DESIGN PROPOSAL · OCTOBER 2026

AI Prosthetic Lab: the whole system

مختبر الأطراف الاصطناعية بالذكاء الاصطناعي: النظام كاملاً

A proposal for the whole system: four clinical domains (prosthetics, orthotics, gait analysis, rehabilitation) on four shared parts (knowledge layer, reasoning and review, search and education, patient record). Every part supports the specialist's decision and never makes it.

  • These are screen designs for discussion, not the working software.
  • All cases and people are invented test data.
  • Citations are shown as placeholders such as “[Guideline], p. [x]”: no source is quoted in these designs.
  • Clinical values, rules and roll-out order are for the owner and the specialists to set.
